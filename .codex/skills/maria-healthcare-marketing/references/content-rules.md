# Content Rules

## Voice

- German by default.
- Use real German characters in visible copy: `ä`, `ö`, `ü`, `Ä`, `Ö`, `Ü`, and `ß`.
- Do not write `ae`, `oe`, `ue`, or `ss` as replacements in German copy. Exceptions: URLs, slugs, code identifiers, file names, env vars, and third-party technical values.
- Calm, clear, warm, competent, and not mystical.
- Patient-facing copy should reduce uncertainty without overpromising.
- Prefer concrete next steps over abstract wellness language.

## Healthcare-Sensitive Copy

Avoid:

- Cure promises or guaranteed outcomes.
- Claims that a method treats, prevents, or diagnoses a disease unless verified and approved.
- Fear-based urgency.
- Before/after claims and patient testimonials without legal review.
- Phrases that imply replacement of emergency care, physician diagnosis, or prescribed treatment.

Prefer:

- "begleitet", "unterstützt", "kann hilfreich sein", "im Rahmen einer individuellen Anamnese".
- Clear boundaries: "Die Behandlung ersetzt keine ärztliche Abklärung."
- Plain explanations of process, duration, preparation, and what happens in a first appointment.

## Conversion Copy

- Primary CTA: appointment or contact request.
- Secondary CTA: learn about services or first visit.
- Trust elements: qualifications, process, transparent limits, calm FAQ, location, accessibility, and contact options.
- Do not invent trust signals. Mark missing data as placeholders.

## CMS Content

Each service page should include:

- SEO title and meta description.
- Slug.
- Short summary.
- Who it may be relevant for, worded cautiously.
- Process.
- What to prepare.
- FAQ.
- Review status.
